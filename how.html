What this is and how it works

A quick read of SoCal flying for the next 7 days: one score per site and day, so you see which day and which site deserve a closer look.

Optimistic on purpose: it shows how good a day can be. Little Black, Blossom, Elsinore and Torrey are tuned against pilots' usual weather tools and local reads; the other sites are educated guesses. Always check the real forecast and conditions on launch.

Reading the page

1 no-go 2 marginal 3 site flying / glass-off 4 XC 5 Great XC day

Day. The coloured word under the date (No-go, Marginal, Site fly, XC day) is the local thermal day at Little Black, Blossom and Elsinore; the colour bar on the left says the same at a glance.

Boxes light up when they apply; tap one to see which sites. Good: XC, glass-off, good height. Bad: low lid, windy, rain, cloud.

Below them: the day type (post-frontal, Santa Ana …, with how many models agree) and the sites worth a look. Tap the day to open it.

Site card. Score, type of flying (Thermal, Cross-country, Ridge soaring, Glass-off), chips from good to bad (tap one for what it means), height of lift, wind on launch and at the top of lift, and the hours from 9 am to 6 pm. Tap the card for what made the score and how sure the models are.

Updates hourly through the day.

What the score means

It rates the best 3 hours between 10 am and the evening. Glass-off, the last 3 hours before sunset, is rated on its own and can lift a day to 3.

Height is the top of usable lift over the launch and the hills around it (maps below). Climb is what you can expect in a good core.

Climb counts in a straight line: 1 m/s gives about half credit, 2 m/s or more full. XC needs the site's XC height, workable wind and at least 1 m/s climb. A 5 needs great height and nothing taken off. The windy chip warns of a steady breeze most of the window (10 mph on launch or 13 at the top); it takes no points.

Sites with their own rules

Torrey: wind decides. A forecast 8 mph from WSW–WNW is soarable, 6–8 marginal, under 6 too light, over 20 too windy. Rain only counts when it's likely.

Elsinore: needs east wind or calm, then the west switch. The card says when it switches, or "no switch".

Laguna / Horse / Fuzz: rates above 2 only when the convergence is there. Horse can also glass off in a light W–SW evening breeze; Laguna can't. The card shows the better launch.

Trip areas (experimental): Santa Barbara / Ojai and Owens; the best launch counts. Owens is for big, calm days only: over 10 mph at the top of lift or 8 on launch makes it 2 at most.

Models

The first 2–3 days use HRRR and RRFS, then RRFS with ECMWF, GFS and ICON. Days 5–7 run on global models only, so expect them to change. The main page lists every source and its run time.

What it doesn't know

Live observations, local effects, turbulence and rotor, overdevelopment, closures, permits and airspace.

Where the radar measures

Wind is read at the launch. Height is also read at points around it, because the best climbs are often over the hills behind launch.
