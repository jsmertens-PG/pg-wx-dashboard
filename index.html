How the rating works
1 no go 2 marginal 3 good local / glass-off 4 XC 5 outstanding

1–5 scale, by site type. Little Black, Blossom, Otay are rated on their best hour in the 11 am–1 pm window, when you get up and out before wind and stability kill it; glass-off is rated separately and the better of the two counts. A nice glass-off or a good local day is 3, a real XC window 4, outstanding 5. Elsinore and Laguna/Horse are XC-only convergence sites: without a convergence setup they cap at 2. Torrey is 2–3 for ridge soaring and 4–5 on a thermal day. Marshall, Santa Barbara (11–3) and Soboba (1–4 pm) use their own windows. Heights come from each site's scale in sites.json (for Little Black: 2,500 ft OK, 3,000 ft XC, 4,500 ft great).

Day type (the coloured pill) comes from the models over the region and runs all 7 days: Front (regional rain, cooling), Post-frontal (the days after, cold air aloft), Pre-frontal (S–SW flow ahead of a front), Santa Ana (NE–E flow at 850 hPa, dry air) and Switch day (the day Santa Ana ends). "maybe" means the models disagree.

Models. While HRRR covers a day, HRRR ×2 and RRFS ×1 set the rating. Once HRRR runs out, RRFS ×2 plus the global models (GFS, ECMWF, ICON together counting once). Past RRFS it is a weighted median of the global models; NAM isn't used. RRFS runs lower than HRRR, so its heights are raised by its recent gap to HRRR at each site before scoring (the heights shown are the model's own, as on XC Skies). ECMWF's wind aloft is shown as a check. The models behind each day are listed at the bottom right of its card. Height bars show RRFS and HRRR (about 4 and 2 days); after that a rough global-model height. The ensembles give the chance of a flyable or XC day. Heights are the top of usable lift, the same idea and close to the same numbers as XC Skies, never above cloudbase; launch wind, gusts (over 15 mph hurts), wind aloft at the top of lift all count. Rain only shows as a "rain x%" tag (share of model and ensemble runs with rain); at 60% or more the day is a no-go. Days the NWS discussion names for showers get an "NWS showers" marker, with no effect on the rating.

Limits for each site are in sites.json, to tune against real days.
