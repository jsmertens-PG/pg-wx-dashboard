Where the radar measures

The wind always comes from the launch itself. For the height of lift, most sites also look at points around the launch, because the best climbs are often over the hills behind it, not right above take-off.

launch (wind)height point (a dot under a launch: the launch itself is measured too)dashed rings every 10 km, north up
How the height is worked out: in the site's flying window the radar reads the top of usable lift at every red point, every hour. It takes the four best readings, at most two from any one point, and averages them. So one strong point counts, but a single spiky hour does not decide the day.
