One region for inland San Diego (Ramona, Alpine, Valley Center, Julian, Jamul, Poway, El Cajon Mtn): the models' hourly values at each point, worked out like the radar (mixed-layer depth from the profile and the model's boundary layer, thermal strength W* from surface heating) and averaged. Each cell is the expected climb at that height in m/s after a paraglider's own sink; clouds mark the cumulus layer. HRRR for today and tomorrow, then GFS. Inspired by XCTherm. Data: Open-Meteo. Updated hourly by a GitHub Action.
