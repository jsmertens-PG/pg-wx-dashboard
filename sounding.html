Balloon path

Cloudbase = surface dewpoint lifted at constant mixing ratio until it meets the temperature curve. Orange lines = dry adiabats (5.4 °F/1000 ft) from the surface every 5 °F, ending at the thermal top for that surface temperature. Zoomed to the flyable range. Blue bands = inversions. Bold line = forecast afternoon high at the launch site (median of the GFS, ECMWF and ICON ensembles); shaded band = 10th–90th percentile of those runs. Updated by a GitHub Action after each 00Z/12Z launch. Thanks Rob!
